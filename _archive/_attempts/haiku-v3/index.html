01 — Where corrections go

Two ways to handle a correction.

Cloud assistant

Your corrections are sent to a server. The provider decides what is stored, for how long, and who can read it.

Tachyon

Corrections stay on your machine. They become a small adapter you own. Delete it and nothing is left behind.

02 — Method

The loop stays on your machine.

01

Capture

Every output you accept, edit or reject is saved locally as a trace.

02

Curate

You choose which traces count. Nothing is learned without your say.

03

Train

A small adapter is trained on the curated traces, on your own hardware.

04

Delete

One action removes the adapter, the traces and the key.
