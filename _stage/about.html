01 — ABOUT

A lab in Brisbane for local-first AI.

Tachyon Lab is an independent research lab. We build AI systems that run on your machine, not in the cloud.

We’re small and hardware-obsessed: the same people who think about models also think about the machines they run on — memory budgets, thermals, and what fits in a desk-side box.

The lab is based in Brisbane, Australia, and works with researchers and engineers elsewhere. If that sounds like your kind of place, get in touch.
